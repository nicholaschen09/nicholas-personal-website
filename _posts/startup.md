## about taking breaks
Long day of work, we were going to our friend's house to hang out, solve puzzles, and get food. Sometimes you forget this, but it is really important that you take breaks when you're working on something full-time and you're not getting paid for it. It just keeps you sane. We're doing this not because someone has asked us to, and that's the best part, but sometimes you still need to go out there and touch grass and see some stranger-things-ahh looking clouds, man.
